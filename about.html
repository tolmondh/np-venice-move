About

This site helps people relocating to North Port or Venice, Florida, with practical, independent checklists. The guides are informational and written to be useful whether or not you ever work with a real estate agent.

It’s built and maintained as a free public resource. Some links (including Amazon Associates packing-supply links) may earn a commission at no extra cost to you; see the affiliate disclosure. We don’t pretend to be the City of North Port, the City of Venice, or Sarasota County.

Questions about a guide? Use the contact path on your preferred channel with the site owner.
