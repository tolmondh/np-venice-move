Moving away from North Port or Venice? Get connected with a vetted agent where you're going

Leaving the area for a new job, family, or a change of pace? Tell me where you're headed, and I'll connect you with a licensed agent there. I'll check their experience, reviews, and how quickly they respond before I introduce you. It's free to you.

How it works

  1. Tell me where you're moving. Email or call with the city, your timeline, and whether you'll buy or rent.
  2. I find a good fit. I look for a licensed agent who works in that area and has a track record with buyers like you. I talk with them first.
  3. You get a warm introduction. I send you their name and contact details and tell them to expect your call. You decide whether to work with them.
  4. I stay in touch. If anything goes wrong, tell me. I'll help sort it out or find someone else.

Selling here before you go?

I can help you sell your North Port or Venice home and line up your agent in the new city, so the two sides of the move are planned together.
