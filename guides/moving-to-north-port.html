Moving to North Port, Florida

A practical newcomer checklist — what to line up before and after you arrive. Confirm every fee and form with the City of North Port, Sarasota County, and your providers.

Not official advice. Rules and portals change. Use this as a planning list, then verify on northportfl.gov and scgov.net.

1. Before you leave

  • Lock housing dates (closing or lease start) and get the exact service address.
  • Ask the landlord/seller which utilities are already in their name and which you must start.
  • Schedule movers or a truck for a weekday if you can — weekend demand spikes in season.
  • Gather IDs, lease/closing docs, pet records, and school transcripts if you have kids.
  • Forward mail with USPS and update banks/payroll addresses.
  • Photograph meter readings on move-out and move-in days if you’re renting.

2. Housing day-of

  • Walk through with a checklist: AC, water heater, locks, screens, irrigation if any.
  • Test outlets and GFCI in kitchen/bath; note anything broken in writing same day.
  • Locate main water shutoff and breaker panel before the first storm season.
  • Confirm HOA / CDD contacts if your community has them (dues, parking, trash rules).

3. Utilities & trash

North Port and Sarasota County split some services. Start here, then use our utilities guide for who to call:

  • Electric — FPL is common in the area; start service with your move date.
  • Water / sewer / trash — often through City of North Port for in-city addresses; confirm for your parcel.
  • Ask about deposit requirements and average first bills so you’re not surprised.
  • If you’re outside city limits, ask Sarasota County which districts apply.

4. Internet

Coverage varies street by street. Before you sign a long contract:

  • Check which providers actually serve your address (not just the ZIP).
  • Compare promo vs year-two pricing and equipment rental fees.
  • If you work from home, ask about upload speeds and outage history in your neighborhood.

More detail: Internet options for North Port / Venice.

5. Florida / county admin

  • Florida license and vehicle registration timelines — check FLHSMV for your situation.
  • Update voter registration if you choose to.
  • Find your nearest urgent care / hospital and save the numbers in your phone.
  • School zoning: confirm with Sarasota County Schools before you assume a campus.
  • Homestead exemption timing if you bought — county deadlines matter.

6. First month

  • Build a small hurricane kit early — don’t wait for the first named storm. See hurricane prep for newcomers.
  • Learn flood / evacuation zone for your address (county maps).
  • Meet neighbors or HOA board; ask about parking, overnight guests, and trash days.
  • Set calendar reminders for HOA dues, insurance renewal, and utility autopay.

7. Printable checklist

  • Housing documents + keys / remotes
  • Electric started
  • Water / sewer / trash started
  • Internet installed or scheduled
  • Mail forwarded
  • License / plates plan
  • Schools / pediatrician if needed
  • Storm kit started
