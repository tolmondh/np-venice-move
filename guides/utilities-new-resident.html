Utilities & trash for new residents

A simple map of who usually handles what. Your parcel may differ — always confirm with the city or county.

Usually separate accounts

  • Electric: Often Florida Power & Light (FPL) in this region — start service with your move date.
  • Water / sewer / garbage: Often city utilities if you’re inside North Port or Venice city limits; otherwise a Sarasota County district or private hauler may apply.
  • Internet / phone: Separate from city utilities — see the internet guide.

Setup checklist

  • Have your service address and move-in date ready.
  • Ask if a deposit or credit check is required.
  • Ask which day trash/recycling runs and whether carts are provided.
  • Save account numbers and autopay login the same week you move.

Start with northportfl.gov, venicegov.com, and scgov.net for current contacts — don’t rely on old blog phone numbers.
