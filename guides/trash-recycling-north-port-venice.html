Trash, recycling & yard waste for new North Port & Venice residents

Who picks up your trash depends on one thing: whether your new address is inside the City of North Port, inside the City of Venice, or in unincorporated Sarasota County (many "Venice" and "North Port" mailing addresses are actually county). Each has its own carts, pickup days, and rules. This page helps you figure out which one you're in and what to do in your first week.

Educational overview only. We don't list fees or exact pickup days because they change and vary by street. Rules below come from the City of North Port, City of Venice, and Sarasota County solid waste pages. Confirm with your provider before you rely on a schedule.

Step 1: Figure out who serves your address

  • Inside North Port city limits: the City of North Port Solid Waste Division collects garbage, recycling, yard waste, and bulk items.
  • Inside Venice city limits: the City of Venice Solid Waste and Recycling Division handles curbside collection.
  • Unincorporated Sarasota County (for example parts of Nokomis, Osprey, South Venice, and areas outside city limits): the county contracts with franchised haulers, Waste Pro of Florida in the North Service Area and FCC Environmental Services of Florida in the South Service Area. Sarasota County has an interactive map on its Trash and Recycling page to look up your provider.

Not sure which applies? Your property tax bill or your water/utility provider usually tells you whether you're in a city or the county. Our utilities guide covers that setup too.

North Port: what newcomers should know

  • Carts: If you bought an existing home, the City says the previous owner should leave the green garbage tote and recycling container at the house (they're registered to the address). For a newly built home, request containers through the City's Solid Waste Online Request Form Center; they're delivered on your next collection day.
  • Frequency: Garbage is collected once a week, and yard waste is collected weekly on the same day as garbage and recycling.
  • One side of the street: North Port uses one-side-of-the-street collection, so your garbage and recycling cart may need to go across the street. Call Customer Service at 941-240-8050 if you're unsure. Yard waste and bulk items stay in front of your own home.
  • Set-out times: Not before 6 p.m. the night before, and by 7 a.m. on collection day, arrows facing the street and about 5 feet from other objects. Bring empty carts back to the garage, side, or back yard by 10 p.m.
  • Yard waste: Bundle, use paper lawn bags, a standard can, or a City yard waste tote (available to lease). The City's yard waste page lists the size and weight limits and what's not accepted, such as lot-clearing debris, sod, dirt, and mulch.
  • Bulk items and appliances: Schedule through the online request center at least one business day ahead (by 4:30 p.m.).
  • Sorting is required: City ordinance says garbage that contains recyclables or yard waste won't be collected.

Venice: what newcomers should know

  • Carts: The City says new residents must get an automated garbage cart from the City if there isn't one at the property, and carts stay with the address when you move. There is a fee for carts; call Public Works at 941-486-2422 to request one or ask about sizes.
  • Frequency: Garbage is collected twice a week; recycling and yard waste once a week.
  • Routes changed in June 2026: The City updated residential routes and dropped Wednesday and Saturday service, so pickup days a seller or neighbor gives you may be outdated. Check the new route map or call Public Works.
  • Set-out rules: Carts curbside by 7 a.m., within 3 feet of the curb and 3 feet from other objects.
  • Recycling: Single-stream, so everything goes in the cart together, loose. No plastic bags, and break down big boxes so they fit.
  • Yard waste and bulk: Weekly yard waste in paper bags, cans, or tied bundles within the City's size and weight limits. Bulk yard waste piles and larger items need an appointment first; see the City's residential how-to guide.

Unincorporated Sarasota County: what newcomers should know

  • Same-day pickup: The county says garbage and recycling are collected on the same day.
  • Set-out times: Out no earlier than 5 p.m. the day before, curbside by 6 a.m., and brought back in by 10 a.m. the day after collection.
  • Need a recycling cart? For a new build or damaged cart, the county says to contact your hauler: Waste Pro (North Service Area) at 941-340-0700 or FCC Environmental (South Service Area) at 941-557-5040.
  • Bulk pickups: Schedule at least 48 hours ahead with your hauler. The county says each eligible residential property gets four free bulk pickups (up to 10 cubic yards) per calendar year; fees may apply beyond that.
  • Recycling is mandatory in Sarasota County.

Moving boxes, old paint, and other move-week leftovers

  • Cardboard: Flatten moving boxes. North Port offers a courtesy extra cardboard request through its online form center; in Venice and the county, break boxes down so they fit in the recycling cart, and hold the rest for the next pickup.
  • Hazardous items (paint, pool chemicals, pesticides, gasoline, batteries, fluorescent bulbs, electronics): these generally aren't collected curbside. Sarasota County residents can take accepted household items free to a Chemical Collection Center, including the South center at 250 S. Jackson Road in Venice. Check the county's hazardous waste page for the accepted list and hours, and bring proof of county residency.
  • Holidays: All three providers skip collection on certain holidays and shift service differently. Look up your provider's current holiday schedule rather than assuming.
  • After a storm: Debris pickup rules change after hurricanes. See our hurricane prep guide and follow your city or county's announcements.

First-week checklist

  1. Confirm whether your address is City of North Port, City of Venice, or unincorporated county.
  2. Check that garbage and recycling carts are at the house; request them if not.
  3. Look up your pickup day(s) from the official map or by phone, not from the listing or a neighbor.
  4. Schedule a bulk pickup for move-in debris if needed.
  5. Set aside paint, chemicals, and batteries for a Chemical Collection Center trip.

Where to verify

  • City of North Port: Garbage and the Solid Waste Online Request Form Center.
  • City of Venice: Waste and Recycling and its residential how-to guide.
  • Sarasota County: Trash and Recycling, including the provider lookup map.
  • Sarasota County: Hazardous Waste.
