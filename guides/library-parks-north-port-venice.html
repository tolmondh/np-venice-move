Library cards, parks & recreation for new North Port & Venice residents

Once the boxes are mostly unpacked, a library card and a parks map are two of the fastest ways to feel settled. North Port and Venice both sit in Sarasota County, so one county library card covers every branch — and each city runs its own parks and recreation programs on top of that.

Educational overview only. Details below come from Sarasota County Libraries’ “How do I get a library card?” FAQ, the Sarasota County Libraries site, City of North Port Parks & Recreation, and City of Venice Parks and Recreation. Hours, fees, and membership rules change — confirm with the library or city before you rely on them.

One library card for the whole county

Sarasota County Libraries and Historical Resources issues the card. If you live in North Port, Venice, or anywhere else in Sarasota County, you’re a resident for library purposes. The same card works at every county branch, including the ones in North Port and Venice, plus digital resources once your account is approved.

How to get a card as a new resident

Per the county’s FAQ, you can apply online or in person.

Apply online

  1. Start at the county’s My Library / get a card page (or the linked online application from the FAQ).
  2. Resident applications are verified through a third party. If you’re approved, the library says you’ll get your card number by email and can use it for the catalog and digital resources right away.
  3. They also send instructions for the Sarasota County Libraries app and adding the card to a digital wallet. You can request a physical card with that number.
  4. If more proof is needed, you’ll get an email with next steps — or call Info Central at 941-861-1110 or visit a branch.

Apply in person

Bring a photo ID and proof of a current Sarasota County address to any county library. The FAQ says a Florida driver license or Florida ID card that already shows your current Sarasota County address can serve as both. Otherwise bring a photo ID plus one residency document such as:

  • Current lease (they list a minimum of six months)
  • Utility bill (water, gas, electric, cable, or satellite)
  • Sarasota County vehicle registration
  • Property tax bill or receipt, or a deed
  • Voter registration card, recent pay stub with address, or other items listed on the FAQ

You can print an application ahead of time or fill one out at the desk.

Kids under 18

A parent or guardian must approve a youth card. Start online if you want, then the parent or guardian brings photo ID and proof of residency to finish it. Spanish-language application PDFs are linked from the same FAQ.

Snowbirds and people who aren’t Sarasota County residents yet

If you don’t qualify as a resident yet (for example, you’re still waiting on a local address, or you’re visiting from out of county), the library offers paid non-resident cards. The FAQ currently lists these non-refundable terms: $15 for 3 months, $25 for 6 months, $40 for 1 year, and $80 for 2 years. Fees are paid through the library catalog with staff help — confirm amounts before you pay.

Residents of some nearby Florida counties may qualify for a reciprocal borrower card for physical checkouts and holds at no cost. That reciprocal option does not include hotspots or online resources (databases, eBooks, eAudiobooks). Reciprocal applicants bring their home library card, a photo ID, and proof of home address. See the FAQ for the participating-libraries list.

Branches near North Port and Venice

Addresses below come from Sarasota County Libraries’ branch listings. Hours change seasonally and by branch — check the county’s Hours and Locations page or call 941-861-1110 before you drive over.

  • North Port Library — 13800 S. Tamiami Trail, North Port, FL 34287
  • Shannon Staub Library — 4675 Career Lane, North Port, FL 34289
  • William H. Jervey Jr. Venice Library — 300 Nokomis Avenue South, Venice, FL 34285
  • Frances T. Bourne Jacaranda Library — 4143 Woodmere Park Blvd., Venice, FL 34293

After you move within the county, the library says you can’t change your physical address online — call or visit with proof of the new address so your record stays current.

Culture Pass (adult resident cards)

Adult residents with a Sarasota County library card can use Culture Pass for free or discounted access to participating local theaters, museums, and similar venues. The library’s FAQ notes Culture Pass is only for adult resident cardholders (not non-resident cards). Sign-up and partner lists change, so start from the Libraries site after your resident card is active and verify what’s available that week.

North Port parks & recreation

The City of North Port Parks & Recreation Department runs community centers, parks, aquatics, camps, and fitness programs. Useful first stops:

  • Morgan Family Community Center (MFCC) — 6207 W. Price Blvd. Gym, fitness center, teen lounge, programs, and outdoor courts. The city describes its “Achieve Anything” membership for facility access; non-members pay daily rates for some drop-in uses. Confirm current membership pricing and fitness-center age rules on the MFCC page.
  • George Mullen Activity Center (GMAC) — 1602 Kramer Way, near City Hall. Gym, meeting rooms, and open-gym schedules. Note: the city says GMAC follows special hours during summer/winter camp and may be closed to the public while camp is in session — call 941-429-PARK (7275) before you go.
  • North Port Aquatic Center — Day passes and annual passes have resident and non-resident rates. Proof of North Port residency is required for resident pricing. See current admission details at North Port Parks & Recreation / the city’s pool pages (often linked as NorthPortFL.gov/Pool).
  • Program registration — Classes, leagues, and camps register through the city’s Parks & Recreation online system from the Community & Recreation hub.
  • Park finder — Use the city’s local parks search (often linked as NorthPortFL.gov/MyLocalParks) for playgrounds, trails, and amenities.

City policy highlights (confirm on the center pages): children under 11 must be supervised by a parent or guardian at MFCC, GMAC, and the Aquatic Center unless enrolled in a program; ages 11+ (or entering 6th grade) need an Achieve Anything membership or a daily fee for teen lounge / open gym access.

Venice parks & recreation

The City of Venice lists more than 30 parks, from pocket parks to Gulf beach access. The city’s Parks and Recreation page and parks directory are the official maps. Newcomers often look for:

  • Venetian Waterway Park — multi-mile trail along both sides of the Intracoastal for walking and biking.
  • Paw Park at Brohard Park Beach — dog park with beach access (city lists it as off-leash dog beach access).
  • Legacy Park, Higel Marine Park, Marina Park — trails, playgrounds, and canoe/kayak or boat launches where listed.
  • Venice Community Center — 326 S. Nokomis Ave. (city/tourism listings). Large community venue for programs, meetings, and events; check the current schedule before you go.

Venice’s parks page also notes tennis and basketball at parks such as Hecksher and Pinebrook, and an interactive parks map with virtual tours for some sites. Always follow posted rules for dogs, boats, and beach access.

First-month settling-in checklist

  1. Apply for a Sarasota County library card online or in person with proof of your new address.
  2. Save your preferred branch and Info Central (941-861-1110) in your phone.
  3. If you’re a North Port resident, look up Achieve Anything / Aquatic Center resident rates and create a Parks & Recreation registration account.
  4. If you’re in Venice, bookmark the city parks directory and Community Center schedule.
  5. Once your resident library card is active, check whether Culture Pass partners interest you.
  6. Update your library address if you change homes inside the county.
