Moving to Venice, Florida

A newcomer checklist for Venice — city vs nearby unincorporated pockets, utilities, and settling in. Verify with venicegov.com and Sarasota County.

Guide · September 2026 · See also Moving to North Port

Tip: “Venice” in listings can mean City of Venice or nearby ZIP areas. Confirm city limits for trash, taxes, and permits.

Before you leave

  • Confirm whether your address is inside city limits.
  • Ask which utilities transfer vs new account setup.
  • Book movers early in peak snowbird season.
  • USPS forward + update critical accounts.

Housing & neighborhood

  • Walk-through notes same day (AC, moisture, locks).
  • Ask about parking, golf carts, and HOA guest rules.
  • If you’re near the beach, ask about flood insurance requirements for your building/lot.

Utilities, trash, internet

  • Start electric (often FPL) for your move date.
  • Confirm water/sewer/trash with the City of Venice or the correct county district.
  • Compare internet by exact address — see internet options.

Full utility map: Utilities & trash for new residents.

First month

  • FL license / registration plan via FLHSMV.
  • Find urgent care and your evacuation / flood zone.
  • Start a basic storm kit — hurricane prep guide.
