HOA first month: a newcomer checklist

Many neighborhoods in North Port and Venice have a homeowners or condo association. Rules, fees, and approval steps vary by community — always read your own association's documents and confirm with the association or its manager.

Educational overview only, not legal advice. We do not quote dues, fines, or approval times. Florida HOA and condo rules are set by your community's documents and state law (Chapters 720 and 718, Florida Statutes); ask an attorney or the association for anything binding.

Documents to gather and actually read

  • Declaration / covenants (CC&Rs): the core rules on use, leasing, pets, vehicles, and exterior changes.
  • Bylaws: how the board is elected, meetings, and voting.
  • Rules and regulations: day-to-day items like trash cans, parking, pools, and guests.
  • Architectural / ARC guidelines: what needs approval before you paint, fence, add a screen enclosure, or install hurricane shutters.
  • Budget and recent meeting minutes: helpful context on reserves and upcoming projects.

Questions to ask the association or manager

  • How and when are dues paid, and is there autopay?
  • Is there a gate, amenity card, or registration for vehicles and residents?
  • What needs approval before move-in, such as moving trucks, PODS-style containers, or elevator reservations in condos?
  • Are there rules for hurricane shutters (when they can go up and must come down)?
  • Renting? Does the association require a tenant application or approval?
  • Who handles landscaping, irrigation, roofs, or exterior insurance, and who handles the rest?

First-month checklist

  • Get the manager's contact info and the community portal login.
  • Register residents, vehicles, and pets if required.
  • Put trash, recycling, and yard-waste days on your calendar (see Utilities & trash).
  • Submit ARC requests before any exterior work, and keep written approvals.
  • Ask your insurance agent how your policy fits with what the association insures (condo "walls-in" vs. HOA single-family differ).
  • Note the next board meeting; attending one early is the fastest way to learn how the community works.

Where to verify

  • Your association's official documents and manager.
  • Recorded documents through the Sarasota County Clerk.
  • Florida's DBPR for condo and HOA resources.
  • City code questions: northportfl.gov or venicegov.com.
