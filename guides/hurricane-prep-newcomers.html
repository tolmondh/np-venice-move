Hurricane prep for newcomers

If you’re new to Southwest Florida storms, build basics early — don’t wait for a named storm in the Gulf.

Follow National Weather Service and county emergency management for live orders. This page is a planning primer only.

Do these in your first month

  • Look up your flood and evacuation zone for your exact address.
  • Know where you would go if told to evacuate (friend inland, hotel plan, or shelter info from the county).
  • Photograph important docs and keep copies in cloud + a zip bag.
  • Stock water, meds, flashlights, power banks, and pet supplies before shelves empty.
  • Ask HOA/landlord about shutter rules and generator restrictions.

Insurance reality check

Confirm homeowners/renters coverage and whether flood is separate. Newcomers often discover gaps after they move — ask your agent early.
