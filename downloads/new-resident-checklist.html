Free new-resident checklist (PDF)

A printable one-to-two-page planning list for people moving to North Port or Venice: utilities and trash, the Florida driver license and vehicle-tag deadlines, voter registration, the March 1 homestead exemption filing date, a library card, and first-season hurricane prep. Built from our longer guides, with pointers to official offices.

This is a planning list, not official government advice. Deadlines, fees, and forms change. Confirm each item with the office named on the checklist before you rely on it.

What’s on the PDF

  • Utilities and trash — start electric, water/sewer, and garbage with the provider that actually serves your address (North Port, Venice, or unincorporated Sarasota County).
  • Driver license and vehicle registration — Florida license or ID within 30 days of establishing residency; title and register the car within 10 days, with Florida insurance first. Handled locally by the Sarasota County Tax Collector.
  • Voter registration — register or update your address in Sarasota County; the deadline is 29 days before an election.
  • Homestead exemption — if you bought a home and will live there, you generally must own and occupy it as your permanent residence on January 1, and file a timely application by March 1 with the Sarasota County Property Appraiser.
  • Library card — one free Sarasota County card works at every county branch, including North Port and Venice.
  • Hurricane prep — official Atlantic season is June 1–November 30; look up your flood and evacuation zone and start a basic kit in your first month.
