How the official rates are calculated

  • Deed documentary stamp tax: $0.70 per $100, or portion thereof, of the consideration (sale price). Round the price up to the nearest $100, then multiply. Minimum $0.70. Sarasota County uses the statewide rate; only Miami-Dade is different. Source: Florida Department of Revenue, Documentary Stamp Tax and section 201.02, Florida Statutes.
  • Owner’s title insurance (promulgated risk premium): For an original owner’s policy, $5.75 per $1,000 on the first $100,000 of liability, plus $5.00 per $1,000 from $100,000 to $1 million, plus $2.50 per $1,000 from $1 million to $5 million, plus $2.25 per $1,000 from $5 million to $10 million, plus $2.00 per $1,000 over $10 million. Any fraction of $100 is treated as a full $100. Minimum premium is $100. The owner’s policy is typically issued for the sale price. Included in this seller estimate only if you set the owner’s policy to seller-paid. Source: Fla. Admin. Code r. 69O-186.003, official rule record at flrules.org, and the Florida DFS Title Insurance Overview.
  • Reissue rates (only if you check the box and the seller pays the policy): $3.30 per $1,000 on the first $100,000, plus $3.00 per $1,000 up to $1 million, with a $100 minimum, if the transaction qualifies under the rule. Confirm with the title company. Source: Rule 69O-186.003(2).
  • Sarasota County recording fees: $10.00 for the first page of each document and $8.50 for each additional page (documents 8.5" × 14" or smaller). This worksheet does not auto-add a seller recording line; enter a figure if you have one (for example, a mortgage satisfaction). Source: Sarasota County Clerk of the Circuit Court and County Comptroller, Recording Fees and Taxes Required.

What this worksheet leaves out or leaves to you

  • Commissions are whatever was agreed in writing. This page will not invent a rate.
  • Prorated property taxes, HOA costs, concessions, title-service fees, and seller recording are included only if you enter an amount.
  • Buyer-side costs (mortgage documentary stamps, intangible tax, lender’s title, deed and mortgage recording) do not reduce the seller’s net and are not shown here. See the closing cost estimator for those lines.
  • Daily interest on a loan payoff, reconveyance or satisfaction fees charged by a lender, attorney fees, and repairs agreed after inspection.
