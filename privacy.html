Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026.

This site is a simple static guide. We do not create user accounts, and we do not ask you to register or log in.

What we collect

This site does not run its own forms and does not collect or store names, emails, or payment details. Hosting (GitHub Pages) and your browser will process standard technical data such as IP address and request logs as part of serving the site — that is controlled by the host, not by a custom backend we operate.

Email newsletter

Our free newsletter, NP & Venice Move Notes, is sent through beehiiv, our email service provider. The “Send me the newsletter” button takes you to beehiiv’s own subscribe page; your email address is entered there and stored by beehiiv to send the newsletter, not on this site. This site itself still does not collect or store your email address. beehiiv’s handling of your information is described in the beehiiv privacy policy.

You can unsubscribe anytime using the unsubscribe link at the bottom of every newsletter email.

Analytics

GoatCounter is not used on this site yet. We may add privacy-friendly analytics later (for example GoatCounter or a similar tool that does not sell personal data and avoids invasive tracking). If we do, this page will be updated to name the tool and what it measures.

Cookies and third parties

Outbound links (including any future affiliate links) go to third-party sites with their own privacy practices. We do not control those sites. See the affiliate disclosure and terms of use.

We do not sell personal data

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We have no advertising network that profiles you across the web through this site.

Contact

Privacy questions (optional): email the site owner at tolmondh@hotmail.com.
